# ✈ AERIS — Airfare Economics & Regional Intelligence System

> A real-time airfare intelligence platform for India that transforms airfare observations into a standardized Airfare Price Index for inflation monitoring, route analysis, and policy-oriented insights.

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 · PS ID 6056 · Travel & Tourism**

---

## Overview

AERIS — Airfare Economics & Regional Intelligence System — collects, validates, normalizes, and analyzes airfare observations across representative Indian domestic routes.

AERIS focuses on measuring the movement of airfare prices as an economic indicator rather than acting as a personalized flight-price predictor.
